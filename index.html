Welcome, Tenno

Warframe is big. This guide is your spoiler-free starting point.

Placeholder — Screenshot: Tenno waking up during Awakening

You are a Tenno — an ancient warrior who pilots a Warframe. Ordis, your onboard Cephalon, and Lotus, the mysterious voice on comms, will guide you through the first hours.

Warframe is free-to-play and grows in layers: first combat and mods, then warframe crafting, then open-world missions, then endgame systems. This site covers the layers in the order you'll meet them.

Spoiler policy

Warframe has a famous story twist. We won't name future quests or locked systems — they show up blurred 🔒 until you complete the matching quest and turn on "Show Spoilers" in the relevant site sections.

Before you continue

If you haven't installed the game yet, do it now — this guide works best with the game running alongside. Set aside 30–45 free minutes to finish Awakening + the first mission of Vor's Prize.

Your first hour

Awakening → warframe pick → Vor's Prize. What to expect and what each step unlocks.

The game opens with you in a stasis chamber. Ordis wakes you up, you fight your first Grineer, and before reaching your Orbiter (the ship) you pick one of three starter warframes. After that, a mission chain called Vor's Prize progressively unlocks the game's systems.

Starter warframe pick

Excalibur
Excalibur
Damage Melee Recommended

Sword warrior with Slash Dash and Exalted Blade. Straightforward kit, scales well, ideal if you want to learn melee combat.

Mag
Mag
Control Magnetic Anti-Corpus

Bends magnetism to pull enemies and strip shields. A great pick against Corpus (the shield faction).

Volt
Volt
Speed Electric Support

Fast, buffs allies with Speed, creates electric shields, and hits crowds with Discharge. Also great mid-game.

Note

You can get all three later — the starter pick just decides which warframe you begin with. No pressure, pick the one whose look you like most.

Vor's Prize — 6 missions, ~40 minutes

1
Retrieve Suit SystemsUnlocks the Arsenal — where you equip weapons, warframe and mods.
2
Retrieve Mods SegmentUnlocks Modding. From here on you can slot mods on your gear.
3
Foundry SegmentUnlocks the Foundry — where you craft new warframes and weapons from blueprints.
4
Nav SegmentUnlocks the Star Chart. Now you choose where to go across the solar system.
5
Craft the Ascaris NegatorYour first Foundry experience. Short timer (a few real-time hours).
6
Assassinate Captain VorBoss fight on Mercury. Rewards: a 3-day Affinity/Credit/Resource booster and access to Earth.
Tip

When Vor's Prize wraps up, start the next Earth quest (Once Awake) right away. Main quests unlock systems — never skip the order.

HUD anatomy

Four screen quadrants + what each symbol means.

Placeholder — Screenshot: full HUD annotated with callouts

Warframe's HUD packs information into every corner. After 1-2 hours you'll tune it out — but at the start it's worth knowing what everything means.

Top-left
Minimap and objective
  • White triangle: you and your facing direction
  • Yellow dot: current objective
  • Green dot: extraction
  • Red dot: enemy (needs a radar aura)
  • Green circle: ally
  • Below: objective progress (enemies left, target HP, timer…)
Top-right
Shields and health
  • Blue bar: shields (regenerate after ~3s without damage)
  • Red bar: health (does NOT regen without a mod)
  • Companion appears right below with its own bars
  • Active buffs and status effects show on the left
  • Press Z to see the squad
Bottom-right
Weapons, ammo, abilities
  • Ammo counter: magazine / reserve
  • Melee: Combo Counter (multiplies Heavy Attack)
  • 4 ability icons → keys 1/2/3/4
  • Light-blue bar: Energy (ability cost)
  • Dots below the crosshair: ability ready / not
Center / crosshair
Target and damage feedback
  • When aiming at an enemy: HP bar above them
  • Segments: blue = shield, yellow = armor, red = health
  • Red arcs around the crosshair: incoming damage direction
  • Damage numbers in different colors = status types
Warning

The HUD gets busy fast once you have multiple buff mods, status auras and an active squad. It's normal — you can shrink the scale under Options → HUD.
